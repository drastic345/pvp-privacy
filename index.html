Privacy Policy for Plants vs Pests

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Plants vs Pests handles information. The game is published by Zearaf Tech. For privacy questions, contact drastic345@gmail.com.

Information handled by the game

Plants vs Pests does not require you to create an account. Game progress and preferences (such as settings and achievements) are saved locally on your device. The developer does not receive this locally saved game data. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

If you choose to share a score or achievement card, the game creates an image and opens Android's sharing interface. The information is sent only to a destination you choose through that interface, and that destination's own privacy policy applies.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to request and show ads. The advertising SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic and performance information, and device or advertising identifiers. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and ad delivery.

The current Android app bundle also contains advertising mediation SDKs. Depending on the mediation and ad-serving configuration, Google and the participating advertising or mediation partners may process information to select, deliver, measure, and protect ads. The partners and data involved can change when the ad configuration changes.

Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy. You can manage Android advertising ID settings through your device settings. The ads shown may depend on your device settings, your location, and the services used to serve the ad.

Data sharing, security, and retention

The developer does not operate an account system or game server for this app and does not receive the locally stored game progress described above. Advertising providers may share or retain information according to their own services, configuration, and policies. Data sent through the Android share interface is handled by the destination you select.

Information stored by the game remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Information handled by advertising or sharing providers is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices. The developer does not control those providers' systems.

Children

Plants vs Pests is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us at drastic345@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, its advertising configuration, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised update date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email drastic345@gmail.com.
